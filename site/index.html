    Sources

    Ticketing feeds

    Ticketmaster, room by room — the Lahaina Civic Center, and the Maui Arts & Cultural Center and the ʻĪao Theater for what their own calendars lack (it lists almost nothing on Maui). Season bundles, parking, suites and tours are left out.

    Venue calendars

    The rooms' own calendars, read daily: the MACC's events listing, Maui OnStage's show cards for the ʻĪao Theater, Hale Hōʻikeʻike's public calendar, ProArts', Mulligans' and the Lahaina Cannery Mall's Events Calendars, Da Playground's ticket shop, the Pacific Whale Foundation's listing, the Maui Invitational's schedule, Hui Noʻeau's exhibitions, the National Park Service's events for Haleakalā and the Lānaʻi Culture & Heritage Center's calendar.

    Game dates

    ESPN for the University of Hawaiʻi Rainbow Warriors — football and basketball — and Rainbow Wahine volleyball, in the Teams view.

    The map

    The islands' outlines are Natural Earth (public domain), drawn here as one file — no map tiles, nothing fetched from anywhere else. Venues are placed from their street addresses.

    The final word

    Refreshed once a day. Every listing links to the venue or ticket page — check there before you go.

    The raw data behind this page, fetchable from anywhere (CORS is open):

    • events.json — JSON feed
    • events.ics — iCalendar feed

    Google and Outlook don't have feed formats of their own — they subscribe to the iCalendar feed. The Add To My Calendar button up top has one-click links for both.
