How It’s Made

Every listing on this calendar is read from the venue’s or the team’s own pages once a day, then checked and tidied by rules we write and keep up by hand.

Choosing the venues

We pick each venue ourselves: the places that put on public events in this city or neighbourhood. For every one we find its own calendar and its ticket seller, and confirm that the page really lists its shows before we read it. No venue is taken from a ticket seller alone. Sources names where this calendar’s listings come from.

The daily build

Once a day, around noon local time, the whole calendar is rebuilt. Each venue’s calendar, its seller’s list and the leagues’ schedules for the home teams are read again, so new shows appear, changed times are corrected and withdrawn shows go. The date of the last build is shown above the list.

What we take out

A raw feed carries a lot that is not an event. We remove parking passes, VIP upgrades and ticket bundles, building tours, and the same show listed twice by a venue and its seller. Titles shouted in capitals are set in ordinary type. These rules are written venue by venue, and we change them when a venue changes how it lists things.

What we check

  • Ticket links are tested, so a listing leads to a real page.
  • “Sold out” and “Nearly sold out” are read from the sellers’ pages.
  • A cancelled or postponed show stays in its place, marked, until its date has passed.
  • Game times a league may still move are flagged.
  • A festival spread over several venues is grouped only after we have looked at it.
  • Once a month every venue link is audited for sites that have moved, closed or been taken over.

When a source breaks

Venues redesign their sites, and a reader that worked yesterday can fail today. When that happens the venue’s last known events stay on the calendar, the failure is reported to us the same day, and we repair the reader.

What has changed

Venues are added, sources are replaced, and now and then a site is merged or retired. The changelog records it.

Corrections

A wrong listing or a missing venue: write to hello@here.events. Say which listing or venue, and we will fix the source it came from.
